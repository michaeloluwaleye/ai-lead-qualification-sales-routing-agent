# AI Lead Qualification & Sales Routing Agent

## AS-IS Process Analysis v1.0

**Organisation:** NexaFlow Solutions  
**Project Type:** Portfolio Simulation  
**Document Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the current lead qualification and routing process at NexaFlow Solutions before implementation of the proposed AI automation solution.

The purpose is to understand how leads currently move through the organisation, identify manual activities and decision points, and establish a baseline for process improvement.

## 2. AS-IS Process Trigger

The process begins when a prospective customer submits their information through a lead-generation channel and a new contact is created in Brevo.

## 3. Current Process

### Step 1 — Lead Captured

A prospective customer submits contact information through a marketing or lead-generation channel.

The contact is recorded in Brevo.

### Step 2 — Salesperson Identifies New Lead

A salesperson checks Brevo and identifies newly received leads requiring assessment.

### Step 3 — Lead Information Reviewed

The salesperson manually reviews available information such as:

- Name
- Company
- Job title
- Contact details
- Company information
- Lead source
- Submitted enquiry or other available information

### Step 4 — Lead Suitability Assessed

The salesperson determines whether the lead appears relevant to NexaFlow's products and services.

The assessment is based on the salesperson's interpretation of the available information.

### Step 5 — Lead Priority Determined

The salesperson determines whether the lead should be treated as:

- High priority
- Medium priority
- Low priority
- Not currently suitable

There is no fully standardised scoring mechanism supporting this decision.

### Step 6 — Follow-Up Action Decided

Based on the salesperson's assessment, they determine what should happen next.

Possible actions include:

- Immediate sales follow-up
- Further manual review
- Future nurturing
- No immediate action

### Step 7 — Lead Information Communicated or Updated

Relevant information is manually communicated to appropriate sales personnel and/or updated within the available business systems.

### Step 8 — Sales Follow-Up

The appropriate salesperson contacts selected leads according to their perceived priority and available workload.

## 5. Current Decision Points

The current process contains three important human decision points:

**Decision 1:** Is the lead relevant to the organisation?

**Decision 2:** What priority should the lead receive?

**Decision 3:** What follow-up action should occur?

These decisions currently depend heavily on manual interpretation.

## 6. Current Process Characteristics

The AS-IS process is characterised by:

- Manual review of incoming leads.
- Human-dependent qualification decisions.
- Limited standardisation of lead scoring.
- Manual prioritisation.
- Manual routing and communication.
- Potential delays between lead capture and sales action.
- Dependence on salesperson availability.
- Limited visibility into the reasoning behind qualification decisions.

## 7. Current Process Baseline

For portfolio simulation purposes:

- Average incoming leads: **100 per week**
- Average manual qualification time: **5 minutes per lead**
- Estimated manual qualification effort: **approximately 8.3 hours per week**

These figures are simulated assumptions and do not represent actual client performance data.

## 8. AS-IS Analysis Outcome

The current process successfully enables sales personnel to assess and follow up prospective customers, but it depends significantly on repetitive manual work and individual judgement.

The AS-IS analysis provides the baseline for identifying specific pain points, determining root causes and evaluating opportunities for process improvement.
