# AI Lead Qualification & Sales Routing Agent

## Stakeholder Analysis v1.0

**Organisation:** NexaFlow Solutions  
**Project Type:** Portfolio Simulation  
**Document Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This stakeholder analysis identifies the key stakeholders involved in or affected by the lead qualification and sales routing process.

The analysis will help ensure that business, user and technical requirements are considered when designing the improved process and automation solution.

## 2. Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role in the Process | Primary Need / Interest | Influence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales Representative | Reviews and follows up incoming leads | Receive relevant, prioritised leads quickly with sufficient information for follow-up | High |
| Sales Manager | Oversees lead management and sales performance | Consistent qualification, appropriate prioritisation and visibility of important leads | High |
| Marketing Team | Generates and captures prospective customer leads | Ensure generated leads are processed efficiently and passed appropriately to Sales | Medium |
| Prospective Customer | Submits information and may require sales follow-up | Receive timely and relevant communication | Medium |
| Automation / AI Specialist | Designs, builds and maintains the automated process | Reliable workflow, accurate data handling, controlled AI decisions and maintainable integrations | High |
| Business / System Administrator | Supports business applications and access | Reliable system integration, appropriate permissions and manageable operational processes | Medium |
| Business Management | Responsible for operational efficiency and commercial outcomes | Improve sales efficiency, scalability and use of organisational resources | High |

## 3. Stakeholder Priorities

The primary stakeholder priorities identified for the project are:

- Reduce unnecessary manual lead assessment.
- Identify high-priority leads more quickly.
- Apply consistent qualification criteria.
- Provide sales personnel with useful lead information.
- Prevent uncertain AI decisions from triggering inappropriate automated actions.
- Maintain human oversight where necessary.
- Improve visibility of lead qualification and routing decisions.
- Ensure the solution remains reliable and manageable.

## 4. Stakeholder Engagement Approach

For this portfolio simulation, stakeholder requirements will be represented through realistic business scenarios rather than interviews with actual employees.

The simulated engagement approach will include:

- Defining stakeholder needs.
- Translating those needs into business and solution requirements.
- Developing user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Testing the completed solution against stakeholder expectations.
- Conducting simulated User Acceptance Testing (UAT).

## 5. Key Stakeholders for UAT

The following stakeholder perspectives will be represented during UAT:

### Sales Representative

- Can qualified leads be understood and acted upon easily?
- Is sufficient information provided for follow-up?

### Sales Manager

- Are leads prioritised and routed consistently?
- Are high-priority and uncertain leads handled appropriately?

### Automation / AI Specialist

- Does the workflow execute reliably?
- Are AI outputs validated before downstream actions occur?
- Are exceptions and failures handled appropriately?

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis Outcome

The analysis identifies Sales Representatives, the Sales Manager and the Automation / AI Specialist as the stakeholders with the greatest direct influence on the design of the lead qualification and routing process.

Their needs will therefore receive particular consideration during requirements definition, process redesign, solution development and UAT.
