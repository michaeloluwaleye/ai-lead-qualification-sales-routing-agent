# AI Lead Qualification & Sales Routing Agent

## Business Problem Definition v1.0

**Organisation:** NexaFlow Solutions  
**Project Type:** Portfolio Simulation  
**Document Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Business Context

NexaFlow Solutions is a fictional B2B technology company that provides business automation and AI solutions to small and medium-sized businesses.

The company generates prospective customer leads through digital marketing and lead-generation activities. New lead information is captured within Brevo and subsequently reviewed by the sales team.

## 2. Current Situation

The current lead qualification process relies heavily on manual assessment.

Sales personnel review available lead information, determine whether a prospect is relevant to the company's services, estimate the priority of the lead, and decide what follow-up action should occur.

As lead volume increases, this manual approach creates operational inefficiencies and makes consistent prioritisation increasingly difficult.

## 3. Business Problem

NexaFlow Solutions lacks a consistent, scalable and timely process for evaluating, prioritising and routing incoming sales leads.

The current manual process may result in:

- Delays in identifying high-value prospects.
- Inconsistent qualification decisions.
- Sales personnel spending time evaluating low-priority leads.
- Important or incomplete lead information being overlooked.
- Potentially valuable prospects receiving delayed follow-up.
- Limited visibility into why particular leads receive specific priorities.

## 4. Business Impact

If the existing process continues as lead volume increases, NexaFlow Solutions may experience:

- Increased sales response times.
- Inefficient use of sales resources.
- Inconsistent lead prioritisation.
- Reduced ability to handle increasing lead volumes.
- Potential loss of sales opportunities because of delayed follow-up.

## 5. Business Need

NexaFlow Solutions requires a more consistent, scalable and timely lead qualification and routing process that helps the sales team identify and prioritise valuable opportunities while maintaining appropriate human oversight for uncertain or exceptional cases.

## 6. Portfolio Simulation Assumptions

For the purpose of evaluating the proposed solution, the following simulated baseline assumptions will be used:

- **Average incoming leads:** 100 per week.
- **Average manual qualification time:** 5 minutes per lead.
- **Estimated manual qualification effort:** approximately 8.3 hours per week.

These figures are simulated assumptions created solely for portfolio demonstration and do not represent actual client performance data.
